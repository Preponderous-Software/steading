How to play

Keep the settlers alive through five winters, or grow to 60 settlers. They gather wood, stone and berries on their own and build huts when there is wood to spare. Nothing grows back in winter.
  • Gather: drag over tiles to have them gathered first (Ctrl+drag or Clear removes the marks).
  • Hut: tap a tile, or right-click it, to plan a hut; it is built as soon as there is wood.
  • Pause Space, speed - +.
  • Fly Tab: WASD move, Q/E down/up, arrows or middle-drag look, wheel to dolly, Shift faster. On a touch screen use Look and the arrow pad.
This browser build saves nothing: closing or reloading the tab ends the colony. The desktop game (source) has saves.
